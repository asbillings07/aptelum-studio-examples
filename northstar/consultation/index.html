Talk through the technology questions taking time away from your business.

Bring the questions.

A first conversation should help you understand your options. No system access or technical audit is needed to begin.

  1. Describe your team and how work happens today.
  2. Identify the interruptions or decisions that keep recurring.
  3. Discuss your current providers, scope and priorities.

Before the conversation.

Understand what a useful next step would involve.

Do we need to change providers first?

No. Understand existing responsibilities, contract terms and transition needs before making that decision.

Should we send passwords or system details?

No. Keep credentials, account access and sensitive technical records out of an introductory enquiry. Any later assessment needs an agreed secure process.

Is the consultation a technical assessment?

The introductory conversation explores priorities and fit. Any technical assessment, service coverage and commercial terms would be scoped separately.
