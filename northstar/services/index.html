Services

The right help.
Clearly understood.

Build a support relationship around your people, systems and priorities. Define what is covered before the first ticket.

01

People & everyday support

Keep the working day moving

Discuss the help your team needs with devices, access and everyday software. Establish support channels, coverage hours, response targets and escalation responsibilities in writing.

02

System care & recovery

Prepare for ordinary disruption

Review maintenance responsibilities, updates, backups and recovery objectives. Actual tools, access and restoration checks depend on the agreed environment; no promise of zero downtime.

03

Projects & technology planning

Make the next decision clearer

Scope a move, replacement or improvement around the business outcome. Clarify the starting state, budget, dependencies, acceptance and handover before changes begin.

Make the boundaries visible.

Is every request included?

No service should hide its limits. Agree routine support, projects, after-hours work, third-party licensing and out-of-scope requests separately.

Do you replace our existing providers?

Start by mapping what each provider owns. Coordination or a staged transition may be more appropriate than replacing everything at once.

How do you handle access?

Establish authorization, named responsibilities and appropriate access before any work. An introductory conversation does not require passwords or access to your systems.

Start with the service you need.

Describe what is getting in the way. We can explore the support model and questions a proposal needs to answer.
