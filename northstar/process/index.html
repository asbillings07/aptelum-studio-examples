What happens
when something changes?

A new location, a growing team or an unexpected interruption can change the plan. Define how decisions are escalated, how work is approved and how you will be kept informed.

What should the handover include?

Named owners, current provider responsibilities, an asset overview, access arrangements, known risks and a practical recovery plan. Sensitive details belong in an agreed secure process.
