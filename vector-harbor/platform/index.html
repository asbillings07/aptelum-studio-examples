Review the delivery questions →

Evaluate the edges.

What are the delivery guarantees?

Ordering, acknowledgment, retries and duplicate behavior require a precise contract and demonstrated evidence. This design example does not assert a delivery guarantee.

How would integration work?

Map your source, destination and authentication boundaries first. SDKs, protocols and compatibility must be verified with the actual product; this mock provides no API.

What happens when a destination fails?

Agree retry bounds, visibility, escalation and recovery behavior before adoption. A replay should be an explicit, controlled operation.

Put your hardest case on the table.

A focused technical conversation should include the interrupted path, not only a successful demonstration.
