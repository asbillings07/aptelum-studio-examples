More focused skin care

Chemical peels

Understand the peel—not just the promise.

Chemical peels use a chemical solution to exfoliate layers of skin. The product and depth matter. A provider should explain the intended purpose, suitability, risks and recovery for the particular option proposed.

What to understand

Product and depth

Ask which peel is proposed and why, including alternatives appropriate to discuss.

Recovery is part of the plan

Understand preparation, healing, sun precautions and provider-approved aftercare before scheduling.

A realistic commitment

Clarify appointments, total cost, follow-up and how to reach the provider with concerns.

A few useful questions

Are all peels alike?

No. Peels vary in depth and recovery. A category name alone is not enough to choose one.

How do I know which peel is appropriate?

Discuss suitability, preparation and recovery with the qualified treating provider before scheduling.

Your complimentary consultation

Discuss your goals and questions without an obligation to proceed. Any treatment or further clinical assessment is separate, with costs explained in advance.
