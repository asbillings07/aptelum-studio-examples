Everyday skin care

Custom facial

A considered pause for your skin.

This appointment centers on facial care: a conversation about your routine, cleansing and product choices, and finishing care explained by the provider. It is distinct from a peel or other medical procedure.

What to understand

A short check-in

Discuss your usual routine and preferences with the provider before the session.

Know what is included

Review the proposed steps and products; do not assume every facial includes the same services.

An understandable finish

Ask what was used and what provider-approved care is appropriate after the appointment.

A few useful questions

Does a facial include a peel?

Peels are a separate service with their own assessment and explanation.

Is a series required?

Discuss any proposed schedule, review points and costs before committing to ongoing appointments.

Your complimentary consultation

Discuss your goals and questions without an obligation to proceed. Any treatment or further clinical assessment is separate, with costs explained in advance.
