Privacy in this example

This is a public website example for design feedback. It has no contact, booking or payment forms, accounts, analytics, advertising trackers or live booking connection. Images and fonts do not load from third-party services.

Appearance saves only your light or dark choice in this browser, shared across the six Wellness and Technology examples. Choose System to remove that saved choice and follow your device setting. This preference is not sent to a server. If browser storage is blocked, your choice applies to the current page only.

GitHub Pages hosts these files and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Its handling of service data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The absence of website analytics does not mean the hosting provider keeps no logs.

Online requests are unavailable in this example. On a client website, an approved booking or contact provider would handle your request under its own terms and privacy notice.

Share design feedback with the person who sent you the link. Please do not include patient, health, account or other sensitive information.
