The story a buyer can follow

Make the value visible.

Recognize the problem

Scattered requests leave the team asking for updates. Begin with that familiar moment so a buyer can recognize whether the problem is theirs.

See the work change

Walk through one request: it arrives, has a clear owner and moves to an explicit next step. Show the mechanism before claiming the benefit.

Understand the fit

Explain what the solution would need to connect with, who would use it and which limits matter. Support claims with actual product evidence when a real business uses this story.

A useful next conversation

Give the demo
a purpose.

A buyer should know what they can learn before agreeing to a call. For this workflow, the conversation could explore their current process, a relevant product walkthrough and the requirements for a good fit.

What should the conversation clarify?
  • Which part of the buyer’s current workflow needs attention?
  • How does the actual product handle that scenario?
  • What integration, access, setup and pricing questions remain?
  • What would a sensible next step involve?

Confirm the real business’s demo format, cost and commitments before publishing an offer.
