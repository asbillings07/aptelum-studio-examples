Product / The flow

Keep the context.
Move the work.

A request needs more than a status. It needs enough context for the next person to act.

One request, made understandable.

Capture the desired outcome, choose a responsible owner and show what is needed next. A missing answer stays visible instead of being hidden behind “in progress.”

01 /

A clear brief

Use a short description, relevant links and a definition of done. More fields are not automatically more clarity.

02 /

An explicit handoff

The next owner sees what changed, what was decided and which questions remain.

03 /

A useful conclusion

Close with the result and a path back to the source, so a finished request does not become another mystery.

What needs a closer look?

How would this fit our existing tools?

List the tools that hold your requests and source documents. A product walkthrough should establish actual integrations, access permissions and migration effort before a team commits.

What happens when the workflow changes?

Walk through a blocked request, a reassignment and a cancellation. Check that the people involved can understand and correct the process.

Can we export our work?

Export formats, retained history and exit steps belong in the product evaluation. No working export capability is provided by this design example.

See the fit in your own work.

Bring a recurring request and the handoffs that make it difficult. A relevant walkthrough starts there.
