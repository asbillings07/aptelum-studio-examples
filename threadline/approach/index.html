Our approach

Work should feel
understandable.

Threadline begins with a simple idea: people do better work when they understand what is needed and have room to use their judgment.

The design question

Can the next person act?

Clear context.
Visible responsibility.
A useful next step.

Structure that
earns its place.

A field, notification or automation is useful only when it helps someone do the work. We favor understandable handoffs over a dashboard that needs constant attention.

01 /

Keep people in control

Make choices, exceptions and corrections understandable. A status change should never substitute for a decision.

02 /

Make room for context

A brief explanation can be more useful than another label. Keep the reason connected to the work.

03 /

Respect the whole experience

Consider setup, learning, recovery and leaving—not only the first successful demonstration.

Ask the awkward questions.

A product conversation is the place to test the fit, including limits and the effort of changing tools.
