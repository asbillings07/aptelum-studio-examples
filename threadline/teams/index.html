For service teams

Pick a process.
Find the friction.

Not every team needs the same workflow. Start with the recurring handoff that keeps generating questions.

01

Client onboarding

Bring the first brief, responsible people and open questions into view. Check what must happen before delivery begins.

02

Creative requests

Connect the desired outcome with a draft, a reviewer and the next decision. Keep revisions connected to their reasons.

03

Internal service

Make it clear who owns a request, what information is missing and how the requester will hear that it is resolved.

Start with one
ordinary week.

Describe how work actually travels today. Where do people ask for updates? Where does an important detail disappear?

A useful evaluation

  • Choose one recurring request type.
  • Include a normal case and a blocked case.
  • Agree what a better handoff would look like.
  • Evaluate effort as well as visible activity.
