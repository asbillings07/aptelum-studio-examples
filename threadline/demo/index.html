Start with the work.

You do not need a polished requirements document. A clear example of today’s process gives the conversation a useful starting point.

  1. Describe one recurring request and who handles it.
  2. Walk through a normal handoff and an exception.
  3. Discuss integrations, access, migration and commercial terms.
  4. Agree whether there is a useful next step.
