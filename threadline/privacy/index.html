Privacy

A website you can explore without submitting information.

This example has no forms, accounts, analytics, tracking scripts or enquiry submissions. Fonts and visual assets are served locally with the page.

The Appearance control saves only your light or dark choice in this browser, shared across the six Wellness and Technology examples. Choose System to remove that saved choice and follow your device setting. This preference is not sent to a server. If browser storage is blocked, your choice applies to the current page only.

The public feedback edition is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting requests may be processed by GitHub under its own terms and privacy practices. This page does not claim that hosting generates no logs.

No demo, consultation or technical conversation can be booked here.
