What to understand

Define the appointment

Understand the appointment’s purpose and the role of the person you will meet.

Separate discussion from treatment

A conversation does not automatically include testing, prescriptions or procedures. Confirm the scope before agreeing to any next step.

Know the next step

Review any proposed referral, follow-up and costs with the appropriate professional.

A few useful questions

Does this include testing or medication?

No. The complimentary initial consultation is a conversation. Testing, medication, procedures and any further clinical assessment are separate and need their own explanation and agreement.

Do I need to choose a program first?

Start with a conversation about your priorities before considering any proposed program.
