Body-care options

Body contouring

Understand the option and its limits.

Non-invasive body contouring uses devices to change the appearance of specific body areas. It is not a weight-loss treatment. Different devices have different intended uses and risks, so the specific proposal matters.

Your complimentary consultation

Discuss your goals and questions without an obligation to proceed. Any treatment or further clinical assessment is separate, with costs explained in advance.

What to understand

The proposed technology

Ask which device and body area are being considered and why.

Suitability and risks

A qualified provider should explain limitations and possible complications before a treatment decision.

The full course

Discuss the number of appointments, realistic expectations, follow-up and total costs rather than a promised transformation.

A few useful questions

Is this a weight-loss program?

No. Non-invasive body contouring does not provide the health benefits of weight loss.

Does non-invasive mean risk-free?

No. Device-based procedures have risks and require an appropriate assessment and provider explanation.
