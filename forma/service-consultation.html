What to understand

Your starting point

Discuss the changes you are considering and the features you want to preserve.

What the proposal includes

The provider explains the options, relevant qualifications, expected costs and follow-up.

Space to decide

Ask questions and take time to consider the explanation. A consultation does not need to end in treatment.

A few useful questions

Is this a treatment appointment?

A consultation is a planning appointment. Confirm its inclusions and fee before arranging any treatment.

Do I have to know which product I want?

No. The consultation is where a qualified provider explains suitable options and their limitations.
