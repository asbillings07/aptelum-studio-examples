What to understand

Set priorities

A few useful questions

Is this a package?

Planning starts with individual priorities. Understand each proposed step and its cost before deciding on a course.

Does planning guarantee a particular look?

No. Outcomes and suitability depend on the actual treatment and individual assessment.
