The appointment, explained

Different treatments. Different purposes.

An injectable consultation starts by distinguishing the kind of change being considered. Product choice and suitability require a qualified treating provider.

Expression lines
Neuromodulators temporarily reduce activity in selected muscles. The conversation focuses on movement, treatment areas and the proposed product.
Facial volume
Dermal fillers address volume and contour. They are a different treatment family, with their own techniques, limitations and risks.
Your treatment proposal
A useful proposal identifies the product, treating provider, areas, price and follow-up. Treatment is separate from the complimentary initial conversation.

A few useful questions

What should I understand before deciding?

Ask about the proposed product, its intended use, alternatives, risks, full cost and follow-up.

Can I select a dose here?

No. Product choice, amount and suitability belong with the qualified treating provider.
